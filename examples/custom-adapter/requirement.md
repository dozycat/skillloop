# Team glossary skill

## Goal
The agent expands internal abbreviations exactly as our team defines them.

## Acceptance criteria
- SLA means Service Level Agreement
- RCA means Root Cause Analysis
- MTTR means Mean Time To Recovery
- SLO means Service Level Objective
- POC means Proof Of Concept
- OKR means Objectives And Key Results

## Constraints
- Answer with the expansion only.
- If an abbreviation is not in the glossary, say so instead of guessing.
